## **How to Log In to ShopBack: Every Method Explained**

ShopBack gives you more than one way into your account, which is both a convenience and a source of confusion. The method you use has to match the method you signed up with — mix them up, and the platform treats you as a stranger.

### **Login Methods Available**

According to ShopBack's official help center, you can log in using any of the following:

- **Mobile number or email address** — the default method, paired with a password you set during sign-up
- **Facebook** — for accounts created or linked via Facebook
- **Apple ID** — for accounts created or linked via Apple's sign-in system
- **Face ID or biometric ID** — available only after you've already logged in through one of the methods above and enabled biometrics in the app's security settings

The key rule: if you signed up with Facebook, you have to log in with Facebook. If you signed up with Apple ID, you log in with Apple ID. ShopBack's help center explicitly notes that "if your ShopBack account was created using other social platforms like Facebook or Apple, you need to log in using the account you originally signed up with." Trying to log in with an email that isn't linked to that social account will fail.

### **Step-by-Step Login on the ShopBack App**

1. Open the ShopBack app on iOS or Android.
2. Tap **Log In** on the welcome screen.
3. Choose your login method — email/mobile + password, Facebook, or Apple ID.
4. If using email or mobile, enter your credentials and tap **Log In**.
5. If you've enabled biometrics, the app may prompt you to use Face ID or fingerprint after your first successful login.

### **Step-by-Step Login on the ShopBack Website**

1. Go to the ShopBack homepage and click **Login / Sign Up** in the top-right corner.
2. Enter your registered email or mobile number and password.
3. Click **Log In**.
4. For social sign-in, click the Facebook or Apple button instead of entering credentials.

### **Step-by-Step Login via the Browser Extension**

The ShopBack browser extension (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) doesn't have its own separate login screen — it shares credentials with your shopback.com web account. Once you install the extension:

1. Click the ShopBack icon in your browser toolbar.

## **ShopBack Login Problems: The Complete Troubleshooting Map**

When ShopBack login fails, the cause almost always falls into one of five buckets. Here's how to identify and fix each one.

### **Problem 1: Wrong Password or Forgotten Password**

This is the most common login failure, and the fix is the simplest. ShopBack's help center confirms the reset flow:

1. On the login screen, tap or click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email address you used to sign up.
3. Check your inbox for a reset email — and check your spam or junk folder if it doesn't arrive within a minute.
4. Click the reset link in the email and set a new password.
5. **Important**: The reset link is valid for **30 minutes only**. If you miss the window, request a new reset email by tapping **Forgot password?** again.

If you're already logged in and just want to change your password, hover over the Account icon next to your Cashback earnings and click **Update password**. You'll need to verify your identity via email or mobile number before the change goes through.

### **Problem 2: Social Sign-In Mismatch**

If you originally signed up with Facebook or Apple ID and now try to log in with an email-and-password combination (or vice versa), ShopBack won't recognize you. The fix is to use the same social account you joined with. If you want to switch to email/password login permanently, you'll need to set a password from within your account settings first, then unlink the social account if you no longer want it connected.

To unlink a social account: go to **Account → Account and security → Login and security**, where you'll see all available login options and the option to unlink any linked accounts.

### **Problem 3: HTTP 401 Error on the App**

The HTTP 401 error is an authentication failure — it means the app can't validate your session. Community troubleshooting points to a few fixes:

- **Force-close the ShopBack app and reopen it** — this clears stale session tokens.
- **Check your device's privacy settings** — on iOS, go to Settings → Apps → ShopBack and verify that privacy permissions aren't blocking the app's network access.
- **Update the app** to the latest version from the App Store or Google Play — older builds sometimes fail against updated backend authentication.
- **Clear the app's cache** (Android) or reinstall the app (iOS) if the error persists.
- **Switch from Wi-Fi to cellular** (or vice versa) to rule out a network-side block.

### **Problem 4: OTP or Verification Email Never Arrives**

ShopBack uses a 6-digit OTP (one-time password) sent to your registered email for account verification, password changes, and certain security actions. When the OTP doesn't show up, ShopBack's help center recommends:

- **Check for typos** in the email address you entered — typos happen more often than people admit.
- **Refresh your email inbox manually** — some webmail clients don't auto-poll for new messages.
- **Check spam and junk folders** — ShopBack's messages sometimes get filtered.
- **Confirm your email provider isn't blocking ShopBack's domain** — corporate and university email systems are the most common culprits.
- **Move to an area with stronger mobile reception** if you're waiting for an SMS OTP instead of email.
- **Update your registered email address** to a different provider if the original simply won't receive ShopBack messages.

### **Problem 5: Account Not Yet Verified**

If you signed up but never completed email verification, you may be blocked from certain actions (like withdrawing cashback) even if you can log in. ShopBack requires email verification via the 6-digit OTP before you can access services such as cashback withdrawal. If you can't verify, the help center suggests updating your registered email to a new address and trying again.

## **ShopBack Account Security: Locking Down Your Login**

A cashback account holds real money, so it deserves the same security hygiene as a banking app. ShopBack offers several layers you can enable.

### **Biometric Login (Face ID / Fingerprint)**

After your first successful password-based login, you can enable Face ID or biometric login in the app's security settings. This lets you skip typing your password on future logins while keeping the account protected by your device's biometric hardware. To activate it, navigate to the security settings inside the ShopBack app and toggle biometric login on — you'll need to be logged in through a standard method first.

### **Email Verification**

ShopBack verifies your email address with a 6-digit OTP during sign-up and again before sensitive actions like withdrawals. Keeping a verified, accessible email on file is what makes password recovery possible, so don't skip this step.

### **Unique Password and 2FA Best Practices**

ShopBack doesn't currently advertise native two-factor authentication in the same way banking apps do, but the OTP-based verification for sensitive actions functions similarly. For maximum security:

- Use a **unique password** for ShopBack — not one you reuse across other shopping or social accounts.
- Keep your **registered email secure** with its own strong password and 2FA enabled at the email provider level.
- **Unlink social accounts** you no longer use from **Account → Account and security → Login and security**.
- **Never share your OTP** with anyone — ShopBack's own security guidance confirms the platform will never ask for your account credentials or OTP.

### **What ShopBack Doesn't See**

Worth knowing for the security-conscious: ShopBack doesn't see your card number, CVV, or billing address. You pay the merchant directly during checkout; ShopBack only receives the affiliate commission data and routes your share back as cashback. ## **Common ShopBack Login Questions, Answered**

### **Can I log in with both Facebook and email?**

Not simultaneously for the same account. If you signed up with Facebook, that's your login method unless you set a password from within your account settings and then optionally unlink Facebook. You can have multiple login methods linked to one account, but you manage them from **Account → Account and security → Login and security**.

### **Why does ShopBack keep logging me out?**

Session timeouts are normal security behavior. If you're being logged out more often than expected, check whether your browser is set to clear cookies on close, whether you're using private/incognito mode, or whether another extension is interfering with ShopBack's session cookies. On the app, a force-close followed by a reopen usually restores the session.

### **I changed my phone number — can I still log in?**

If you signed up with a mobile number and no longer have access to it, you'll need to update your registered number from within your account settings (while you're still logged in) or contact ShopBack Care for assistance. This is why keeping a verified email on file matters — email-based password recovery works even if your phone number changes.

### **Is ShopBack login safe?**

ShopBack uses standard authentication practices, doesn't store your card details, and complies with CCPA in the US. The OTP verification for sensitive actions adds a layer beyond a simple password. As with any financial-adjacent account, the main risk is on the user side — reused passwords, shared OTPs, and unverified email addresses are the most common ways accounts get compromised. 👉 [Sign up securely and start earning cashback](https://bit.ly/ShopbacK)

### **What happens to my cashback if I can't log in?**

Your cashback stays in your account. Login problems don't affect your balance — Pending and Confirmed Cashback remain intact while you resolve the login issue. The only risk is missing out on new cashback during the period you can't log in, since the platform can't attribute click-throughs to an account you're not signed into.
